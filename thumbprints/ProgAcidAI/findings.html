Your production signature

Six habits the data keeps showing

Filtering

You carve every synth from below

Nearly every synth filter is a high-pass: Acid Bass at 56 and 148 Hz, High Keys at 540 and 723 Hz, Moody Melody band-pass then 879 Hz, Lead at 1 kHz, Shimmer at 1.15 and 2.36 kHz. Brightness changes come from moving those cutoffs, not from closing low-passes.

Low end

Only the kick and toms own the bass

Kick sits at 60 Hz with 99% of its energy below 250 Hz; Toms at 110 Hz. Acid Bass has just 6% of its energy in the 60–250 Hz band. Its home is 250 Hz–2 kHz.

Acid

The squelch is drawn by hand

761 cutoff points and 420 resonance points ride a resonant high-pass on the Tranceport preset across bars 1–143. The MIDI under it is whole-bar held notes; the preset's own sequencer makes the 16ths.

Harmony

Pedal tones, motion from the patch

A minor (0.88 from the mix audio). A holds 63% of all note time. Melodic tracks hold notes for 4 to 32 beats and let Berlin Sequence, 7 Osc Chord Rave Seq and Live's Arpeggiator generate the line. Intervals move in 4ths, 5ths and octaves.

Groove

Straight kick, tribal everything else

Four-on-the-floor kick at a flat velocity 99; toms on the off-beat 8ths with velocity 61 ± 37; claps on 2 and 4 with a ghost 16th before 4; a Brazilian afoxé shaker; flat 16th hats; cowbell 8ths at 127.

Space

Wide synths, mono drums, dark top

Lead's left/right correlation is −0.07 and the melodies sit between 0.09 and 0.51, while kick, claps and hats are at 0.99 or higher. High-mid plus air is under 3% of the whole mix.

Track reference

The thumbprint, track by track

Attack is the median 10%→peak rise of each hit; decay is the time to fall 20 dB. Width is side energy relative to mid (0 dB is very wide, below −25 dB is mono).

Sound families

What sounds like what

Each thumbprint carries a 48-number vector: 31 third-octave levels, 12 timbre coefficients (MFCCs), brightness, noisiness, attack, decay and width. Cosine similarity on that vector groups your tracks like this.

Method and limits

How this was measured

  • MIDI, automation, devices and presets were read from ProgAcidAI.als without changing it. Analog Lab preset names, packs, authors, tags and descriptions were decoded from the plugin state saved in the set.
  • Stems were rendered by Live (All Individual Tracks, bars 1–160, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit) without return or main effects, so the mix figures describe the sound before your Glue and Color Limiter. The mix peaks at 0.0 dBFS at that point.
  • Per-track pitch is reported as the strongest pitch classes, because overtones bias key detection on a single instrument. The key comes from the full mix, and the MIDI agrees.
  • Two tracks have no clips in the arrangement and rendered silent: Decorations and 22-Analog Lab V (Blue Universe).
  • Files are in F:\Ableton\ReggaeBuilder Project\ProgAcidAI Analysis\: thumbprints.json, ANALYSIS.md, the raw extractions, spectrogram images and the rerunnable pipeline. Stems are in ProgAcidAI Stems\.
